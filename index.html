bodg·er / ˈbɒdʒ ər /
noun

A digital interface or software application designed to intercept and reframe "fexting" (text-based conflict) into constructive dialogue, or "connects-ting."

Etymology

Derived from the archaic British term bodger—a highly skilled itinerant woodworker who turned unseasoned timber into functional chair legs using a pole lathe. In a modern context, the name suggests the "turning" of raw, "green" (reactive) emotion into considered, constructive outreach.
