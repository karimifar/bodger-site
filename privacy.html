Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026
The short version: Bodger does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. Messages you process are sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses and are not stored by us. Any history you choose to save stays on your device. If you opt in, you can help improve Bodger by anonymously sharing message texts and reframes with us — no personal information is attached.

What Bodger does

Bodger is an iOS app that helps you soften reactive messages before sending them and decode hurtful messages you receive from others. It uses AI to reframe texts to be constructive, relational, and respectful.

Information we collect

Bodger does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The only information the app handles is:

Message text you enter

When you type or paste a message and tap Soften or Decode, that text is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. It is transmitted securely over HTTPS. We do not store this text on any server.

Text history (optional, local only)

If you turn on "Save text history" in the app, processed messages are saved locally on your device using iOS secure storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can view and permanently delete your history at any time inside the app.

Filter improvement data (optional, opt-in only)

If you choose to opt in, Bodger will occasionally send us the message text you entered and the reframe it generated. This data is submitted anonymously — no account, device identifier, name, or other personal information is attached. We use this data solely to evaluate and improve Bodger's filter. You can opt in or out at any time in the app's settings.

Third-party services

Bodger uses Anthropic's API to process messages. When you use the app, your message text is sent to Anthropic's servers to generate a response. Anthropic's handling of this data is governed by their own privacy policy and terms of service, available at anthropic.com/privacy.

Bodger does not use any advertising networks, analytics services, or other third-party data processors.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect usage analytics or crash data
  • We do not use advertising identifiers
  • We do not share any data with third parties beyond Anthropic (for API processing)
  • We do not retain copies of messages you process (unless you have opted in to filter improvement sharing)
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not access your contacts or messages

iMessage extension

Bodger includes an optional iMessage extension. It operates under the same privacy principles as the main app: text you process through the extension is sent to Anthropic's API and is not stored by us. Any locally saved history is shared between the extension and the main app via iOS's secure App Group storage, which remains on your device. Filter improvement opt-in settings apply to the extension as well.

Children's privacy

Bodger is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. We'll notify beta testers of significant changes via TestFlight.
